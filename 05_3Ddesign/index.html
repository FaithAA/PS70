<div class="textcontainer"> <p class="margin"> </p> <h3>Week 5: 3D Design & Printing</h3> <h4>Assignment: Model and 3D print something</h4> <br> <p class="margin"> For the first assignment, I 3D Scanned teeth. I am not sure whose teeth these are, but I found them in lab and scanned them. They appear to have been 3D printed. </p> <iframe id="vs_iframe" src="3Dscan.stl" style="border:0;margin:0;width:100%;height:300%;"></iframe> <br> <p class="margin"> It was quite a challenge to get the teeth right. The main reason was because of how meshing works. The base was included in the scan, thus required to manually delete the unwanted parts. For those parts I wasn't able to delete, they carried on after the mesh. I like the results (it was my third trial on 3D scanning). The initial object I was 3D printing was extra detailed, and resulted in not so nice prints (or very not accurate), comparing to the real object. It was also very round, shiny, whcih I think was talked about in lecture why it isn't ideal to scan very reflective surfaces. But, the teeth, they turned out to pripnt easily and nicely because they aren't very much detailed, and they are a perfect surface for 3D scanning. </p> <br> <p class="margin"> For the other assignment that consisted of 3D printing something, I chose to make a small assembly to get practice on how elements fit together. I wanted to make just a standing chill guy that can move legs around. To be able to have these movements meant that I had to print these components separately. The product consists of 4 components total, and they are connected with holes and screws. </p> <br> <p><b>Meet the chill guy</b></p> <div> <img src="3dprint1.JPG" alt="a small design of a robot" style="width: 400px; height: 250px; object-fit: cover; display: block; margin: left;"> </div> <p>Other positions of the chill_guy</p> <div> <img src="3dprint2.JPG" alt="placeholder for you about me image" style="width: 450px; height: 300px; margin: left;"> </div> <br> <div> <img src="3dprint3.JPG" alt="placeholder for you about me image" style="width:450px; height: 300px;"> </div> </div> <p class="margin"> Click the link below to see the revised final project idea <h4><a href="./13_finalproject/index.html">Final Project</a></h4>
